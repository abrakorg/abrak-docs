> The figures on this page can change. **<https://abrak.org/pricing> is always the
> reference** — that calculator shows exactly the number that gets invoiced.

## The principle: per user, not per app

The standard plan gives access to **all 82 apps**. You pay per **internal user**.
Switching on another app costs nothing extra.

That differs from the common model of selling each module separately, and the
difference shows up when you want to add Inventory alongside Accounting.

## Who counts

Only **internal users** — the people who log into the back office.

Suppliers, customers and portal users are **not counted**. A shop with 5 staff and
5,000 customers pays for 5 users.

## Billing periods

Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual. Annual billing includes **two free months**.

There is also an **unlimited** option: any number of users at a fixed price — useful
when the team is large enough that counting seats becomes the problem.

## Tax

VAT of **10%** is added at payment.

## Support

Basic support is included with every subscription. If you need a faster answer, you
choose a higher tier separately at a published rate:

| Tier | First response | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Within 24 working hours | Free — included with every subscription |
| Silver | Within 8 working hours | Per user, per month |
| Gold | Within 2 working hours | Per user, per month — includes phone support |

Final amount = rate x number of users x months in the billing period. No hidden costs.

## The seven-day trial

The first seven days are free, with no bank card and full access. You decide at the end.

## Bundles

Sets of apps that cost less together than the same apps bought separately. Listed on
<https://abrak.org/pricing>.
