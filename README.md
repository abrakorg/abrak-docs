## What Abrak is

Abrak is the real Odoo 19 core — not a fork — packaged as a hosted service with the
pieces an Iranian business actually needs: the Jalali (Solar Hijri) calendar, a
right-to-left Persian interface, Rial pricing through Iranian payment gateways,
in-country hosting, and connectors to the systems local businesses already use.

**82 integrated apps** across accounting, CRM, inventory, manufacturing, HR, point of
sale, e-commerce and marketing. Each tenant gets its own database. You pay per user,
not per app.
